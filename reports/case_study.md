# Case Study: E-Commerce Orders Analysis

## Problem Statement
Analyze customer purchasing behavior, top products, and regional revenue in an e-commerce dataset.

## Dataset Summary
- Customers, Products, Orders, Order Details
- Sample dataset for demonstration

## Key Queries & Insights
- Total revenue per customer
- Top-selling products
- Revenue by region
- Customer ranking by revenue (using window functions)

## Business Takeaways
- Identify high-value customers
- Optimize product stock and categories
- Focus marketing in high-performing regions

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates end-to-end SQL analysis using PostgreSQL.
